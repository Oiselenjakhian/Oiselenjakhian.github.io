Ise

This is a simple Mancala type game! Similar to Ayo and Oware.

How to Play

Welcome to Ise! The objective is to collect more seeds in your store than your opponent.

  1. Each player controls six pits on their side of the board.
  2. Select one of your pits to pick up its seeds.
  3. The seeds are distributed one by one into subsequent pits by moving in a counter-clockwise direction.
  4. In the process of gameplay, the number of seeds in a pot can reach up to 4. Once this happens, the seeds are captured. Capture can be direct or incidental.
  5. With direct capture, the sowing ends in a pot that already has 3 seeds. When this occurs, the seed that would be added makes the number of seeds in that pot equal to 4. As a result of this, the player who did the sowing will get the seeds that are captured assigned to the player.
  6. With incidental capture, the sowing during the course of the game increases the number of seeds in a pot to 4. The seeds that are captured are then assigned to the owner of the pot.
  7. The grand finale occurs when only 8 seeds are left on the entire board. At this point the first person to capture wins the remaining 4 seeds.
